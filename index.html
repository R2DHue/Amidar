How to play

Travel along the grid, collect the dots, and outline every box to paint its interior. The board scrolls vertically as you move.

  • Move: far-right joystick, arrows / WASD, or controller D-pad / left stick. Four directions only; no diagonals.
  • You keep moving in your last valid direction after releasing the controls, just like the original. Hold your next direction until you reach a junction.
  • Jump: far-left button, Space / X, or controller A / B. You have three jumps per life. While jumping, enemies appear to leap and cannot hurt you. They do not stop moving.
  • Score: 10 per dot; 500 for a bonus. Paint all 22 boxes to advance. Three lives; jumps refill after a death or new level.
  • Start: Jump, Enter, controller Menu, or Start. P / controller View pauses. Escape returns to the title.

Original board, sprites, and lettering from Paul Robson’s May 25, 2001 build 335. Browser rules translation; newly designed contemporary sound, no sampled music. Best in landscape on iPad. Sound starts only after your first interaction.
